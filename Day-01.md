## Questions

- What is the Site Editor?
  A Page is like writing one chapter.
  A Post is like writing a blog article.
  The Site Editor is like designing the book itself—its cover, page layout, fonts, and navigation.
  
- How do themes work?
  A theme controls how your website looks, not what your content is.
